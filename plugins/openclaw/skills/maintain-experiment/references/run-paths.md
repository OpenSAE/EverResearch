## Name every experiment path with meaningful words

Apply this rule to every segment beneath `experiment/`, including dataset and model branches, execution folders, framework-generated folders, and output filename stems. Each segment contains at least one complete English word that describes its actual content or role. Use descriptive words joined with underscores for project-defined names. A scientific identifier can accompany a descriptive word when it conveys a real dataset or model identity.

Reject date or time information in any segment. Reject opaque IDs, hashes, UUIDs, random suffixes, and names composed solely of numbers or abbreviations. Adding a descriptive prefix does not make a date, timestamp, or opaque suffix acceptable.
